# Black Duck Statement on Inclusivity and Diversity

Black Duck Software, Inc. is committed to creating an inclusive environment where every employee, customer, and partner feels welcomed.

We are reviewing and removing exclusionary language from our products and supporting customer-facing collateral.
Our effort also includes internal initiatives to remove biased language from our engineering and working environment,
including terms that are embedded in our software and IPs. At the same time, we are working to ensure that our web
content and software applications are usable to people of varying abilities. You may still find examples of non-inclusive
language in our software or documentation as our IPs implement industry-standard specifications that are currently under review to remove exclusionary language.
