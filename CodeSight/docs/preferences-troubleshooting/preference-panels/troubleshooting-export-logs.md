# Troubleshooting / Export Logs

Use the Export Logs button when you need to
investigate a problem with Black Duck Support.

Figure 1. Troubleshooting panel, IntelliJ preferences
  
 [image: Troubleshooting panel for IntelliJ preferences]

In Visual Studio, this control appears on the Authentication preferences panel.

In VS Code, the title of the panel is simply Export Logs, and the link to display this panel appears on the
HELP view.
