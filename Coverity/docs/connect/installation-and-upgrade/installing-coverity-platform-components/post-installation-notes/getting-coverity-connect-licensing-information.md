# Getting Coverity Connect licensing information

Coverity Connect licensing information is provided in the Help menu, in
the About option in Coverity Connect.

You can verify the type of license by clicking the About link in
Coverity Connect and examining the License Packaging field. To
request information about the restrictions of your license, or to inquire about
upgrading your license, contact your Black Duck sales representative.
