# Installing Coverity Reports

Coverity Reports are installed separately from Coverity Connect. You can obtain
installers from the Downloads page in Coverity Connect. For more
information on configuring and running Coverity reports, see Configuring and Generating Coverity Reports.

1. In Help > Downloads,
   select the Coverity Reports installer that is appropriate for your
   operating system.
2. Save the installer application to an appropriate folder on your system.
3. Launch the installer, and follow the instructions in the wizard.
