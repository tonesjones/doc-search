# The Coverity deployment maturity model

The deployment maturity model provided by Coverity consulting services is a program that
takes your deployment's level of maturity into consideration and works with you to craft
a roadmap towards successful adoption and integration of development testing into your
development life cycle. Beginning with a comprehensive understanding of your business
goals, use cases and technical environment, Coverity Professional Services will create a
deployment plan based on a level-by-level assessment that aligns with your
organizational objectives.
