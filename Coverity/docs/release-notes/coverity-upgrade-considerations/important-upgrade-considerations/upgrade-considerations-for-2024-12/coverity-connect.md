# Coverity Connect

Starting with Coverity 2024.12, the administrative account for Coverity Connect must
adhere to a strong password policy upon installation.

When upgrading from an earlier version to Coverity Connect 2024.12, the strong password
policy for the administrative account will not be enforced, unless the administrator
specifically decides to change the existing password.

Note: The strong password policy means that the password
needs to be at least 8 characters long and contain at least one digit, as well as
lowercase, uppercase, and special characters. We recommend a strong password policy
for all Connect users.
