# Inclusion of Cybersecurity

Beginning with the 2022.6.0 release, Coverity will maintain a yearly certification
process for ISO/SAE 21434.

In addition to the failure modes detailed in this manual, Coverity customers should
implement the following security best practices:

- Do not run Coverity Analysis as a root user.
- Follow the instructions in the "Securing Coverity Connect" section of the
  Coverity Platform User and Administrator Guide.

For additional information, contact your Black Duck Account Team to obtain a copy of the
ISO/SAE 21434 certification report.
