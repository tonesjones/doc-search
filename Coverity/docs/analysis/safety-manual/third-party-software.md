# Third-party software

Coverity includes third-party software described in the Legal notice.

**Black Duck makes no claim about comprehensive correctness of the third-party software
packages it includes with Coverity**, though it performs extensive testing of
these third-party packages within the context in which they are used. Additional
third-party packages are included in the products as necessary, and updates to the
third-party packages are made as a result of known enhancements from which they can
benefit, or due to quality or security enhancements made in the third-party tools.
