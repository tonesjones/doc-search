# Managing your Detect Desktop scans

The **History** tab lets you view and manage scans that were created on your local
machine—particularly useful when working in offline mode or reviewing scans before you
upload.

To open and use the **History** tab:

- Click [image: History icon] .

A list of previously saved scans appears in the left-hand column.

From this tab, you can:

- **View scan details**: Select a scan to review metadata such as scan status,
  operations performed during the scan, and the Detect parameters used for the
  scan.
- **Locate the scan file on your system**: Click **Show Files** to open the
  file location on your machine.
- **Delete a scan**: Hovering over a scan in the list and click [image: Trash bin icon] . Click **Yes** to confirm.
