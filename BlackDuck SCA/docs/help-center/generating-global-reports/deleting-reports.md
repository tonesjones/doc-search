# Deleting reports

To delete a report:

1. Click [image: Trashcan] in the row of the report you wish to delete.
2. Click **Delete** in the confirmation dialog box.

Note the following:

- Reports older than 30 days are automatically deleted.
- The system retains up to 20 reports, per user, across all project versions. If a
  user creates more than 20 reports, the system automatically deletes the oldest
  reports and retains the 20 newest reports.
