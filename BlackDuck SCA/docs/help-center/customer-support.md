# Customer support

If you have any problems with the software or the documentation, please contact Black Duck
Customer Support:

- Online: <https://community.blackduck.com/s/contactsupport>
- To open a support case, please log in to the Black Duck Community site
  at <https://community.blackduck.com/s/contactsupport>.
- Another convenient resource available at all times is the [online Community
  portal](https://community.blackduck.com/).
