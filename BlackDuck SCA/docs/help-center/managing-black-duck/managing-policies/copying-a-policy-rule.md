# Copying a policy rule

Users with the Policy Manager role can
copy policy rules.

To copy a policy:

1. Click [image: image] > **Policies**.

   The Policy Management page appears.
2. Click [image: Options] in the row of the policy you want to copy and select **Copy**.
3. Add the information for this policy and click **Create**.

   The policy name is the only required field.
