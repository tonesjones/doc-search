# Activating or Deactivating SBOM and VEX Templates

By default, templates are set to active after creation. A deactivated template will not
appear as a selectable option when generating a report.

- Activating or deactivating a SBOM template
- Activating or deactivating a VEX template

Note:

**Required permissions:** You must have Custom Fields Administrator role
permissions to delete templates.

Note: The System (built-in) templates cannot be deactivated.

## Activating or deactivating a SBOM template

Note that you can deactivate a SBOM template at any time.

To activate or deactivate a SBOM template:

1. Click [image: Manage] and then select **SBOM and VEX Templates**.
2. Click the **SBOM** tab.
3. Enable the **Active** switch in the box of the desired SBOM template:

   - [image: Active icon] indicates the SBOM template is active.
   - [image: Inactive icon] indicates the SBOM template is inactive.

## Activating or deactivating a VEX template

Note that you can deactivate a VEX template at any time, with one exception: the
current default template cannot be deactivated. To deactivate it, first set a
different template as the default.

To activate or deactivate a VEX template:

1. Click **Manage** and then select **SBOM and VEX Templates**.
2. Click the **VEX** tab.
3. Enable the **Active** switch in the box of the desired VEX template:

   - [image: Active icon] indicates the VEX template is active.
   - [image: Inactive icon] indicates the VEX template is inactive.
