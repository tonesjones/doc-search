# Change your organization's default policies

As an Organization Admin, you can update the default policies assigned to applications, projects, and branches in your portfolio.

Changes to organization-level policies automatically apply to all applications, projects, and branches that are in the Inherited state. Levels in the Modified state are not affected.

Note: Only Organization Admins can complete these steps.

1. Go to My Organization > Policies.

   A table that shows how your default policies are applied to branches appears under Default values for new branches.
2. Select Edit next to the type of policy you want to modify.
3. Add, remove, or change policies, as required.
4. Select Save.
5. (Optional) Repeat these steps to adjust other types of policies, as required.

The updated policy is applied to all inherited applications, projects, and branches in your organization.
